`@fragiola/tree-angular`: the Tree primitives as Angular standalone directives on your own elements (`[treeRoot]`, `ng-template[treeItems]`, `[treeItem]`, `[treeItemContent]`, `[treeGroup]`, `[treeToggle]`, `[treeCheckbox]`, `input[treeRenameInput]`, `[treeDropIndicator]`, `[treeLiveRegion]`, the `TREE` array), the injection functions (`injectTree`, `injectTreeItem`, `injectTreeState`) and the whole core re-exported. Signals, zoneless, two-way `[(expandedItemIds)]`, `[(selectedItemIds)]` and `[(checkedItemIds)]`; the same ARIA and `data-*` as the React and Vue adapters.
