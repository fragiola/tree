The same word always means the same thing, in the API, in these pages and in the examples.

## Items

- An **item** is a node of the tree: an `id`, a `kind`, a `label` and its `data`.
- A **leaf** is an item with neither `childItemIds` nor `childrenStatus`; any other item is a
  **parent**.
- A **root item** is an item at the top level (`rootItemIds`); its **level** is 1.
- A parent's **children status** says where its children come from when your loader provides
  them: `unloaded`, `loading`, `loaded` or `error`. A parent without one holds its children
  itself.
- An item's **label** is raw data: the app renders it, the packages never do.
- An item's **kind** is the key of your registry that types its **data**
  ([Typed data](/docs/concepts/typed-data)).

## States

- **Expanded**: its children are shown. **Collapsed** is the opposite.
- **Selected**: part of the selection. The **selection mode** is `none`, `single` or
  `multiple`; the **selection anchor** is where a range selection (Shift) starts.
- **Checked**, **unchecked** or **mixed**: its checkbox. In `cascade` mode a parent is mixed
  when only some of its descendants are checked; in `independent` mode each item is checked on
  its own.
- **Focused**: the roving tab stop, the one item with `tabindex="0"`. It is model data, kept when
  the tree does not have DOM focus.
- **Renaming**: its label is being edited.
- **Loading**: its children are being loaded.
- **Disabled**: focusable, not actionable. A disabled item is not expanded, selected, checked,
  renamed, moved or dropped into.

## Permissions

- **Movable**: the item can be moved (`item.move`, a drag).
- **Renamable**: its label can be edited.
- **Droppable**: a parent that accepts items moved into it.

Each flag is set on an item or defaults from the tree's `settings`.

## Drag and drop

- A drag has **dragging items** and a **drop target**: a parent, an index among its children,
  and a **position** relative to the hovered item: `before`, `after` or `inside`.
- The **drop indicator** shows the drop target: a line before or after a row, or the row's box
  for `inside`.
- A **keyboard drag** steps through the same drop targets with the arrows.

## The tree

- **Visible items** are the items in render order whose ancestors are all expanded, each with its
  `level`, `posinset` (its position among its siblings, from 1) and `setsize`.
- A **path** is an item's position from the root: `/0/2`.
- The **model** is the tree's data and its rules; a **command** is the only way to change it; a
  **middleware** runs around every command. The **engine** is one tree on screen.
